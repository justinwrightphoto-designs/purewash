Built on Doing Things Right

Purewash is a local, family-focused exterior cleaning company based in Franklin, TN. We started this business because we believed there was a better way to clean — one that didn't require spraying harsh chemicals on the homes where families live.

A Local Business, Built Around Your Family

We're a small, owner-operated company based in Franklin, TN. Greg started Purewash with a simple idea: pressure washing and soft washing should leave your home looking great — and leave nothing behind that could harm your kids, your pets, or your yard.

Most pressure washing companies reach for chlorine bleach because it's cheap and fast. But chlorine doesn't just clean — it lingers. It kills grass. It can irritate skin. And the smell alone tells you something isn't right.

Our hydrogen peroxide-based formula is different. It breaks down naturally after doing its job. No residue, no harsh smell, no tradeoffs. Just a clean home that's actually safe to walk around the next day.

We serve Franklin, Nashville, Brentwood, and the surrounding Williamson County area. We work 7 days a week, we respond fast, and we don't leave until the job looks right. If something needs a touch-up, we come back.
