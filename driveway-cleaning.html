Driveway Cleaning

Restore your concrete and pavers. We remove oil stains, algae, tire marks, and years of embedded grime — leaving your driveway looking like itself again.

Concrete and Pavers Can Handle Real Pressure

Unlike home siding or roofing, concrete and paver surfaces are built to take it. Professional-grade pressure washing is exactly the right tool here — and the results speak for themselves. Oil stains, algae, mold, tire marks, dirt, and buildup that's accumulated over years gets lifted out of the surface, not just pushed around.

We use hot or cold water depending on what the surface needs, and we apply a pre-treatment to loosen stubborn staining before washing. The combination makes a real difference, especially on older driveways with deep grime.

What We Clean

  • Concrete driveways and aprons
  • Brick and paver driveways
  • Sidewalks and walkways
  • Patios and pool decks
  • Oil stains and tire marks
  • Algae, mold, and green growth

Results You Can See from the Street

Driveway cleaning is one of the most visually dramatic services we offer. The difference between a cleaned and uncleaned concrete surface is immediately obvious — and it adds real curb appeal. Whether you're preparing your home for sale or just want it to look its best, a clean driveway makes an outsized impression.

How Long Does It Take?

Most residential driveway cleans take 1 to 2 hours. Larger driveways or surfaces with heavy buildup may take a bit longer. We'll give you an estimate when you call. You don't need to be home — just make sure we have access to water.
