Roof Soft Washing

Low-pressure roof cleaning that removes algae, moss, and bacteria without lifting or cracking a single shingle — and keeps your roof cleaner for longer.

ROOF SOFT WASHING — Before & After Photo

Why High Pressure Is Wrong for Roofs

Asphalt shingles are designed to shed water, not absorb it at high velocity. Pressure washing a roof strips granules from the surface of shingles — the protective layer that reflects UV and sheds moisture. Once those granules are gone, your shingles age faster, your warranty may be voided, and you're looking at a roof replacement years ahead of schedule.

Soft washing uses a fraction of the pressure and lets the cleaning solution do the actual work. The result is just as thorough — and your roof is completely unharmed.

What's Actually on Your Roof

Those dark streaks running down your roof aren't just dirt or discoloration. They're Gloeocapsa magma — a type of algae that feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles. Left untreated, it spreads, holds moisture against the shingles, and accelerates shingle decay. Moss and lichen do the same thing.

Our nontoxic cleaning solution kills algae, moss, and bacteria at the root level — not just on the surface. That's why a properly soft-washed roof stays clean for years, not months.

Safe for Your Landscaping

A common concern with roof cleaning is runoff — the cleaning solution running down and pooling near your flower beds, shrubs, or grass. Our hydrogen peroxide-based formula breaks down safely and won't harm your plants. We also take care to pre-wet landscaping before and rinse after, as an added precaution.

How Long Does It Take?

Most roof soft washes are completed in 1 to 3 hours depending on roof size and pitch. The cleaning solution needs a short dwell time to do its work. You'll notice significant improvement immediately, with full results visible over the following days as remaining algae dies off and washes away with rain.
